ABOUT THE ARTIST

THE ARTIST

Finding beauty
in simple details.

Gauri Bhagwat is an artist who explores creativity through drawing and visual expression.

Her work is shaped by careful observation, patience and an appreciation for detail. Each drawing is approached as an opportunity to capture an idea, a feeling or a moment through her own artistic language.

This collection presents a selection of her work, created with a simple intention: to turn imagination and observation into something that can be seen and remembered.
